Safari Overview

This 6day itinerary offers a practical, well-spaced tour through Western Uganda’s key national parks. Planned specifically for a private group of nine across different age groups, the schedule keeps daily road trips manageable while covering chimpanzee trekking, crater lake walks, savannah game drives, and boat safaris.

Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Ziwa Rhino Ranch to Kibale National Park

Meet your Sea Land Safaris driver-guide at Ziwa Rhino Ranch after breakfast to start the drive west toward Fort Portal. The route passes through rural towns, farm country, and tea fields. We will make regular stops for lunch, coffee, and stretching. Arrive in the Kibale area by late afternoon, check into your hotel, and spend the rest of the day resting before the next morning's trek.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Mountains of the Moon Hotel

Day 2: Chimpanzee Trekking & Bigodi Wetland Walk

Head to the park headquarters after breakfast for a brief orientation before entering Kibale Forest. Tracking chimpanzees usually takes between 2 and 4 hours, depending on where the troops moved overnight. Along the trail, guides will also point out birds, plant species, and smaller primates like grey cheeked mangabeys. Return to the lodge for lunch and a short rest.

In the afternoon, head to Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for an easy paced walk through the boardwalks and community paths. The wetland is known for sitatunga antelopes, red-tailed monkeys, and bird species like the Great Blue Turaco. Return to the Hotel for dinner.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Mountains of the Moon Hotel

Day 3: Kibale – Lake Nkuruba – Kasese – Queen Elizabeth National Park

After breakfast, make a short transfer to Lake Nkuruba Nature Reserve. This crater lake is surrounded by forest trails where black-and-white colobus and red colobus monkeys are frequently spotted. The walk here is flexible and kept on flat ground to suit everyone in the group.

Continue through Kasese town for lunch before entering Queen Elizabeth National Park. Check into your lodge, and then head out for a late-afternoon game drive across the Kasenyi plains to look for elephants, buffalo, Uganda kob, and lions.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Bush lodge

Day 4: Queen Elizabeth Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Start early with a morning game drive when animals are active before the heat of the day. Your guide will track wildlife across the tracks, looking for lions, hyenas, elephants, and plain game. Head back to the lodge mid-morning for breakfast and time to relax.

After lunch, transfer to the Mweya jetty for a 2-hour boat trip along the Kazinga Channel. The channel links Lake George and Lake Edward and has heavy concentrations of hippos, Nile crocodiles, and waterfowl, alongside elephants taking water along the banks.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Bush lodge

Day 5: Queen Elizabeth to Lake Mburo National Park on 18/Sept/2026

After breakfast, check out and begin the drive east toward Lake Mburo National Park, traveling through the rolling hills of Mbarara. Reach the park in time for lunch at the lodge.

The afternoon is kept light: you can relax at the lodge enjoying the views or take a short, relaxed drive into the park to view herds of impalas, zebras, and elands.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, DinnerOvernight: Igongo Cultural centre

Day 6: Lake Mburo Game Drive – Equator Stop – Entebbe Airport Drop-off

Take a final morning game drive through Lake Mburo to spot giraffes, zebras, topis, and bushbucks. After the drive, return to the lodge to check out and start the return trip toward Entebbe.

Stop at the Equator line in Kayabwe for lunch, craft shopping, and photos. Continue straight to Entebbe International Airport, arriving in time to check in for your evening flight back home.

Meals: Breakfast, LunchDrop-off: Entebbe International Airport
