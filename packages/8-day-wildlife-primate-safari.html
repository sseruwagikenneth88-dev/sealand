Explore Uganda with Sea Land Safaris

This 8-day safari takes you through Uganda’s most diverse landscapes, combining savannah wildlife, river experiences, crater lakes, and primate encounters. The journey begins in Entebbe and leads you through Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, Murchison Falls National Park, Fort Portal, Kibale Forest, and Queen Elizabeth National Park before returning to Entebbe.

✓ Close encounters with the Big Five – lions, leopards, elephants, buffalo, and rhinos
✓ Chimpanzee tracking in Kibale National Park – home to 13 primate species
✓ Rhino tracking at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary
✓ Game drives in Uganda’s most iconic parks: Murchison Falls, Queen Elizabeth & Ishasha
✓ Boat cruises on the River Nile & Kazinga Channel, teeming with wildlife
✓ Top of Murchison Falls viewpoint – where the Nile explodes through a narrow gorge
Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Entebbe to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary- Rhino Tracking

Welcome to Uganda, the Pearl of Africa! Upon your arrival, a professional SEA LAND SAFARIS guide will warmly welcome you and pick you up from your preferred location in Kampala. Your adventure begins with a scenic drive through the lush countryside as you head north toward the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary.

Arrive at the lodge, check in, and enjoy a refreshing lunch. After lunch, embark on an exciting guided rhino tracking experience inside the sanctuary. This rare opportunity allows you to get remarkably close to the endangered southern white rhinos while learning about the conservation efforts that protect them.

As you walk through the vast plains accompanied by trained rangers, expect to encounter other wildlife species like antelopes and beautiful birdlife. Later return to the lodge and spend the rest of the afternoon relaxing, taking in the tranquility of the sanctuary. Later, enjoy a delicious dinner served in a serene bush environment before retiring for the night.

Activities: Rhino trackingAccommodation: Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 2: Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to Visit Top of the falls

After breakfast, depart from Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary and drive to Murchison Falls National Park (2 hrs). En route, visit the Top of the falls, where the mighty Nile River plunges 43m through a 7m gorge. Feel the ground tremble beneath your feet as you witness the power of the world’s longest river.

After check-in at the lodge and, embark on an evening game drive across the savannah. Expect to see elephants, giraffes, Jackson’s hartebeest, and with luck, lions or leopards or Spend the afternoon relaxing at the lodge, perhaps by the swimming pool.

Activities: Top of the Falls & evening game driveAccommodation: Fort Murchison lodge/Pakuba Safari Lodge(Budget & Midrange),Nile Safari Lodge/Entikko Safari lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board

Day 3: Game Drive and Boat cruise on Nile River

Start early (6:30 am) with a sunrise game drive on the Buligi Circuit, one of East Africa’s most scenic safari routes. Look for predators like lions returning from a night hunt, herds of buffalo, Rothschild giraffes, Uganda kobs, and warthogs.

After lunch, take a boat cruise on the Nile to the base of Murchison Falls. Along the banks, watch hippos wallowing, crocodiles sunbathing, and elephants drinking. Birdlife is spectacular – keep an eye out for the rare shoebill stork. Later proceed to the Lodge as you do an evening game drive. Enjoy dinner and rest for the night.

Activities: Morning game drive and boat cruiseAccommodation: Fort Murchison lodge/Pakuba Safari Lodge(Budget & Midrange),Nile Safari Lodge/Entikko Safari lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Red chilli Restaurant

Day 4: Scenic Drive to Fort Portal

After breakfast, you will begin your journey to Fort Portal, a town known for its scenic beauty and proximity to the Rwenzori Mountains. The drive takes you through changing landscapes, from savannah to greener highlands.

You will have lunch at Gardens Restaurant, a well-known spot offering a relaxing atmosphere and good meals. After lunch, you will proceed to your accommodation at Rwenzori Guest House, where you can relax and enjoy the peaceful environment.

Accommodation: Ruwenzori Guest house/Isunga Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Kyaninga Lodge/Crater Safari lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board (Lunch at Gardens Restaurant)

Day 5: Crater Lakes Region and Transfer to Kibale

After breakfast, you will explore the crater lakes region around Fort Portal. You will visit the historical site of Amabere ga Nyina Mwiru, where you will learn about local legends and cultural history while enjoying views of small waterfalls and caves.

You will then continue to Semuliki National Park to visit the hot springs. Here, you will witness boiling water emerging from the ground, a fascinating geothermal phenomenon set within a lush forest environment. Enjoy lunch on the way.

Later in the day, you will transfer to Kibale National Park, known as the primate capital of East Africa. Upon arrival, you will check in at Kibale Forest Camp and relax for the evening.

Activities: Visit Amabere ga Nyina Mwiru and the hot springsAccommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Isunga Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge/Crater Safari lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board(Lunch on the way)

Day 6: Chimpanzee Trekking and Bigodi Swamp Walk

You will start the day early with breakfast before heading into the forest for chimpanzee trekking. With the guidance of experienced rangers, you will walk through the forest in search of chimpanzees. Once located, you will spend time observing them as they feed, play, and interact. Their loud calls and energetic movements make this a very exciting and immersive experience.

In the afternoon, you will visit Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for a guided swamp walk. This community run project offers an opportunity to see a variety of monkeys, birds, and other wildlife, while also supporting local conservation efforts. You will also interact with the local community and learn about their way of life. You will return to your lodge for dinner and overnight.

Activities: Chimpanzee tracking, Bigodi swamp walk and Visit the local community.Accommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Isunga Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge/Crater Safari lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 7: Kibale to Queen Elizabeth National Park

After breakfast, you will transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park, one of Uganda’s most popular wildlife destinations. Upon arrival, Enjoy lunch and you will proceed for an afternoon boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, which connects Lake Edward and Lake George. This area is rich in wildlife, and you will see large concentrations of hippos, buffalo, and elephants along the water’s edge, as well as a wide variety of bird species.

In the evening, you will go for a game drive as the sun begins to set. This is a good time to spot predators such as lions becoming active for the night, as well as other animals moving across the plains. You will then head to Bush Lodge for dinner and overnight.

Activities: Afternoon boat cruise and Evening game driveAccommodation: Bush Lodge/Twin Lakes safari Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Elephant Plains Lodge & Mweya Safari Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 8: Morning Game Drive and Return to Entebbe

You will wake up early for a final game drive in the park. This last opportunity allows you to see animals you may have missed, including hyenas, elephants, antelopes, and possibly lions.

After the game drive, you will begin your journey back to Entebbe. The drive offers scenic views of the countryside and gives you time to reflect on the safari experience. You will have a lunch stop along the way before continuing to Entebbe, where you will be dropped off in the evening.

Activities: Morning Game driveMeal Plan: Lunch on the way
