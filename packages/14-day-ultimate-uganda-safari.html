Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Uganda – Entebbe

Arrive at Entebbe International Airport, where our professional guide will warmly welcome you. Transfer to your lodge for check-in and relaxation after your flight. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a short briefing about your safari.

Accommodation: Avilion HomeMeal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 2: Mabamba Swamp Bird Watching Experience

After breakfast, head to Mabamba Wetland for an exciting bird-watching excursion. This wetland is one of Uganda’s best birding destinations and is famous for the rare Shoebill Stork.

Enjoy a guided canoe ride through the swamp channels while spotting different bird species including papyrus gonoleks, African jacanas, kingfishers, herons, and more. The peaceful environment and beautiful scenery make this a rewarding nature experience.

Activities: Bird watching, canoe rideAccommodation: Avilion Home & 2 Friends beach Hotel (Budget & Mid -range), Entebbe Forest Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 3: Transfer to Jinja – Source of the Nile Experience

After breakfast, travel to Jinja, Uganda’s adventure capital. Visit the famous Source of the River Nile where the world’s longest river begins its incredible journey northward.

Enjoy a relaxing boat ride to the exact point where the Nile starts from Lake Victoria. Depending on your interest, optional activities include white-water rafting, quad biking, horseback riding, tubing, or simply relaxing by the river.

Activities: Source of the Nile visit, optional adventure activitiesAccommodation: Home on the Nile/The haven Eco River Lodge (Budget & Mid-range), Kalagala falls/Lemala Wilwaters Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 4: Transfer from Jinja to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

After breakfast, depart from Jinja and travel to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary, the only place in Uganda where wild rhinos can be seen in their natural habitat.

Upon arrival, enjoy an exciting guided rhino trekking experience on foot accompanied by experienced ranger guides. Get up close to these magnificent giants while learning about rhino conservation efforts in Uganda. The sanctuary is also home to other wildlife species including oribis, bushbucks, monkeys, and numerous bird species.

After the activity, relax at the lodge and enjoy the peaceful natural environment as you prepare for the next day’s journey to Murchison Falls National Park.

Activities: Rhino trekkingAccommodation: Ziwa Rhino and Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 5: Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park – Top of the Falls & Evening Game Drive

After breakfast, depart from Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary and continue your journey to Murchison Falls National Park, Uganda’s largest and most famous national park.

Upon arrival, visit the spectacular Top of the Falls where the mighty River Nile squeezes through a narrow 7-meter gorge before crashing powerfully 43 meters below, creating one of the most dramatic natural wonders in Africa. Enjoy the breathtaking views, thunderous sounds of the falls, and great photography moments.

Thereafter, proceed to the lodge for check-in and relaxation. Later in the evening, head out for an evening game drive across the savannah plains in search of elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, hartebeests, lions, and other wildlife species.

After the game drive, return to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

Activities: Top of the Falls visit, evening game driveAccommodation: Fort Murchison Lodge/Pakuba safari Lodge (Budget & Midrange), Nile Safari Lodge/Paraa Safari Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 6: Game Drive & Nile Boat Cruise

Begin the day with an early morning game drive across the savannah plains of Murchison Falls National Park in search of elephants, giraffes, lions, buffaloes, Uganda kobs, hartebeests, and leopards.

In the afternoon, enjoy a scenic boat cruise on the River Nile to the base of Murchison Falls. This is one of Uganda’s most rewarding wildlife experiences, offering opportunities to see hippos, crocodiles, elephants, and a wide variety of birds along the riverbanks.

Later return to the lodge for dinner and relaxation.

Activities: Morning game drive, Nile boat cruiseAccommodation: Fort Murchison Lodge/Pakuba safari Lodge (Budget & Midrange), Nile Safari Lodge/Paraa Safari Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 7: Transfer to Fort Portal via Scenic Countryside

After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive to Fort Portal, passing through beautiful landscapes, tea plantations, and local villages.

Fort Portal is one of Uganda’s most beautiful towns, surrounded by crater lakes and the Rwenzori Mountain ranges.

Accommodation: Ruwenzori View GH/Isunga Lodge (Budget & Mid-range),Kyaninga Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 8: Sempaya Hot Springs & Amabere ga Nyina Mwiru Experience

After breakfast, head out for a memorable excursion to Semuliki National Park to visit the famous Sempaya Hot Springs, one of Uganda’s most fascinating natural attractions. Witness boiling hot water bubbling from deep underground and learn about the local cultural beliefs and legends surrounding the springs. You may even have the opportunity to see eggs and bananas being boiled in the naturally hot water.

Later, return to Fort Portal and visit the historical Amabere ga Nyina Mwiru caves and waterfalls, a site rich in ancient Bachwezi legends and cultural history. Enjoy the scenic surroundings, beautiful waterfalls, crater lakes, and lush vegetation while learning about the traditional stories connected to the caves.

After the excursions, return to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

Activities: Sempaya Hot Springs visit, Amabere ga Nyina Mwiru caves & waterfalls, cultural storytelling, nature experienceAccommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Chimpanzee Forest Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 9: Chimpanzee Trekking & Bigodi Community Experience

After an early briefing, enter Kibale Forest for chimpanzee trekking. Watching these intelligent primates swing through the forest canopy is truly unforgettable.

In the afternoon, visit Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary for a community-guided walk. Experience local traditions including coffee processing, basket weaving, herbal medicine, music, dance, and storytelling.

Activities: Chimpanzee trekking, community walk, cultural experienceAccommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Chimpanzee Forest Lodge(Budget & Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 10: Transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park via Lake Nkuruba

After breakfast, visit the beautiful Lake Nkuruba Crater Lake known for monkeys, birds, and scenic views.

Proceed to Queen Elizabeth National Park and enjoy an evening game drive in search of elephants, antelopes, warthogs, lions, and other wildlife.

Activities: Crater lake visit, bird watching, evening game driveAccommodation: Bush Lodge/Aardvark Safari Lodge(Budget & Midrange)Mweya safari lodge/Elephant plains Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 11: Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Enjoy a morning game drive through Queen Elizabeth National Park’s diverse ecosystems. Wildlife sightings may include lions, elephants, buffaloes, waterbucks, hyenas, and many bird species.

In the afternoon, take a boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel, famous for its high concentration of hippos and crocodiles. The cruise also offers excellent bird watching and scenic photography opportunities.

Activities: Morning game drive, Kazinga Channel boat cruiseAccommodation: Bush Lodge/Aardvark Safari Lodge(Budget & Midrange)Mweya safari lodge/Elephant plains Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 12: Transfer to Bwindi & Batwa Cultural Experience

After breakfast, travel through the scenic Kigezi Highlands to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Later, enjoy a Batwa cultural experience where the Batwa people share their traditional forest survival skills, hunting techniques, music, dances, and storytelling. Learn about the history and lifestyle of Uganda’s indigenous forest community.

Activities: Batwa cultural experienceAccommodation: Ride 4 a woman/Bakiga Lodge(Budget& Mid-range)/Mahogany springs, Four Gorillas Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 13: Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi – Relaxation & Island Exploration

After breakfast, proceed to the breathtaking Lake Bunyonyi, surrounded by terraced hills and dotted with numerous islands.

Spend the day relaxing in the cool weather, enjoying canoe rides, island exploration, nature walks, and community interactions. This peaceful destination is perfect for relaxation after days of adventure.

Activities: Relaxation, boat ride, island explorationAccommodation: Bunyonyi overland Resort/Bird nestResort(Budget&Midrange),Arcadia Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast

Day 14: Transfer from Lake Bunyonyi to Entebbe

After breakfast, enjoy the beautiful morning views of Lake Bunyonyi before beginning your journey back to Entebbe.

Drive through the scenic rolling hills, rural villages, and beautiful countryside landscapes of southwestern Uganda. En route, make a stop at the Uganda Equator for lunch, photography, souvenir shopping, and a brief experience standing in both the Northern and Southern Hemispheres at the same time. Later proceed to Entebbe for your departure flight or drop-off location.

Activities: Scenic drive, Equator stop, photography, souvenir shoppingAccommodation: Day room on request / DepartureMeal Plan: Bed & Breakfast
