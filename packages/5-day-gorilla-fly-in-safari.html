Explore Uganda with Sea Land Safaris

This 5-day Uganda Gorilla Fly-in Safari offers an unforgettable experience

to Uganda's famous Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, home to the

endangered mountain gorillas. The safari combines comfort and adventure,

including a scenic domestic flight, gorilla trekking, and a cultural

encounter with the Batwa community.

✓ V Return domestic flights (Entebbe - Kihihi - Entebbe)
✓ V Two gorilla trekking experiences in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park
✓ V Batwa cultural experience
✓ V Scenic views of southwestern Uganda
✓ V Comfortable accommodation and personalized transfers
Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Arrival at Entebbe International Airport

Upon your arrival at Entebbe International Airport, our representative will

warmly welcome you and transfer you to Avilio Home for relaxation, dinner,

and an overnight stay.

Entebbe Forest Lodge /Lake Victoria Serena Golf Resort & Spa (Luxury)

Accommodation: Avilio Home/2 friends Beach Hotel (Budget & Mid-range),Meals: Dinner

Day 2: Flight to Kihihi and Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

After breakfast, you will be transferred to Aerolink for your scheduled

domestic flight to Kihihi Airstrip. Upon arrival, your driver/guide will be

waiting to transfer you to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Check in at your booked lodge, enjoy lunch and spend the rest of the day

relaxing and preparing for the gorilla trekking experience.

Midrange), Buhoma Lodge /Mahogany springs Lodge (Luxury)

Accommodation: Ride 4 a Woman Lodge/Buhoma community haven (Budget &Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 3: First Gorilla Trekking experience and Batwa Community Visit

After an early breakfast, you will proceed to the park headquarters for a

briefing before embarking on the once in a lifetime gorilla trekking

experience.

After spending one memorable hour with the mountain gorillas, return to the

lodge for lunch. In the afternoon, enjoy a cultural visit to the Batwa

community and learn about their traditions and way of life. Return to the

lodge for dinner and overnight.

Midrange), Buhoma Lodge /Mahogany springs Lodge (Luxury)

Accommodation: Ride 4 a Woman Lodge/Buhoma community haven (Budget &Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 4: Second Gorilla Trekking Experience and Afternoon at Leisure

After an early breakfast, return to the park headquarters for another

briefing before setting out for your second gorilla trekking experience.

This provides a unique opportunity to visit a different gorilla family and

enjoy another memorable encounter with these magnificent primates.

After trekking, return to the Lodge for lunch. Spend the afternoon relaxing

at the lodge, enjoying its peaceful surroundings, beautiful gardens, and

community projects, or simply unwind after your adventurous mornings in the

forest.

Dinner and overnight at the lodge.

Day 5: Transfer to Kihihi and Flight to Entebbe

After breakfast, transfer to Kihihi Airstrip for your return flight to

Entebbe. Upon arrival in Entebbe, our representative will transfer you

either to Entebbe International Airport for your onward flight or to your

preferred hotel.

Transportation Arrangement

Vehicle 1: Bwindi Operations Vehicle

. Travels from Kampala to Kihihi Airstrip on day 1 before the guest's

arrival.

. Receives the guest at Kihihi Airstrip and transfers her to Bwindi

Impenetrable National Park on day 2.

. Provides all transportation during the stay in Bwindi, including both

gorilla trekking excursions and the Batwa community visit.

. Transfers the guest back to Kihihi Airstrip on Day 5 for the return

flight.

Vehicle 2 - Entebbe Operations Vehicle

. Provides airport pick-up upon arrival at Entebbe International Airport

to Avilio Home on day 1.

. Transfers the guest from Avilio Home to Aerolink for the domestic

flight on day 2.

. Receives the guest upon return from Kihihi and transfers her to the

airport or hotel of choice on day 5.

Meals: Breakfast
