Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Entebbe to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary

Welcome to Uganda, the Pearl of Africa! Upon your arrival, a professional SEA LAND SAFARIS guide will warmly welcome you and pick you up from your preferred location in Kampala. Your adventure begins with a scenic drive through the lush countryside as you head north toward the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary.

Arrive at the lodge, check in, and enjoy a refreshing lunch. After lunch, spend the rest of the afternoon swimming pool and relaxing, taking in the tranquility of the sanctuary.

Later, enjoy a delicious dinner served in a serene bush environment before retiring for the night.

Accommodation: Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 2: Shoebill Trekking Expedition and Rhino Tracking

After an early breakfast, set out for an exciting Shoebill Trekking experience—one of the most sought-after birds encounters in Africa. Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary is one of the few places in Uganda where the majestic Shoebill Stork can reliably be seen in its natural wetland habitat.

Your trek begins with a guided walk through the sanctuary’s lush vegetation and into the swampy areas where the Shoebill is commonly found. Depending on water levels, the experience may include a short canoe excursion deeper into the marsh, increasing your chances of spotting this prehistoric-looking bird.

The Shoebill, known for its towering height, powerful bill, and slow, graceful movements, is a rare and unforgettable sight. As you explore the wetlands, keep an eye out for other spectacular water birds, including herons, egrets, African jacanas, and swamp-dwellers that thrive in this ecosystem.

After your Shoebill Trek, return to the lodge for lunch and later in the afternoon embark on an exciting guided rhino tracking experience inside the sanctuary. This rare opportunity allows you to get remarkably close to the endangered southern white rhinos while learning about the conservation efforts that protect them.

As you walk through the vast plains accompanied by trained rangers, expect to encounter other wildlife species and beautiful birdlife. spend the afternoon relaxing around the swimming pool or enjoying the peaceful sanctuary surroundings.

Enjoy dinner and overnight at the lodge.

Activities: Shoebill Trekking (Wetland walk + canoe exploration depending on the season) and Rhino trackingAccommodation: Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 3: Transfer to Murchison Falls National Park – Visit Top of the Falls

After breakfast, check out and set off for the magnificent Murchison Falls National Park—the largest protected area in Uganda. Your journey takes you through beautiful rural landscapes, open plains, and local communities. Upon arrival, proceed straight to the Top of the Falls, where the mighty River Nile squeezes through a narrow 7-meter gorge before crashing 40 meters below with incredible force. The sight and sound of the water roaring through the rocks create an awe-inspiring moment.

After the visit, continue to your lodge and enjoy lunch. Spend your afternoon relaxing by the swimming pool with panoramic views of the Nile. Later, in the evening return to the park for an evening game drive.

Enjoy your dinner and unwind for the evening.

Activities: Visit to the Top of Murchison Falls and evening game driveAccommodation: Fort Murchison Lodge /Pakuba Safari Lodge (Mid-range), Paraa Safari Lodge /Nile Safari Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 4: Sunrise Game Drive & River Nile Boat Cruise

Begin your day with an early breakfast before heading for a thrilling sunrise game drive across the northern sector of the park—an area renowned for dense concentrations of wildlife. Look out for Elephants, Rothschild’s giraffes, Buffaloes, Lions, Hartebeests, Warthogs, Waterbucks, Uganda kobs.

After the game drive, enjoy lunch at Red Chilli Restaurant. In the afternoon, embark on a scenic boat cruise along the River Nile. This tranquil 2–3 hour cruise provides exceptional views of hippos, crocodiles basking on the riverbanks, magnificent bird species, and dramatic scenery—culminating at the base of the powerful Murchison Falls. After the cruise, enjoy an evening game drive en route to the lodge and settle in for the night.

Activities: Morning Game Drive, Boat Cruise and evening game driveAccommodation: Fort Murchison Lodge/Pakuba Safari Lodge (Mid-range), Paraa Safari Lodge /Nile Safari Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Red Chilli Restaurant

Day 5: Return to Entebbe

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast before checking out. Begin the drive back to Entebbe, passing through picturesque landscapes, small trading towns, and rolling countryside. Lunch Enroute at Kabalega diners in Nakitoma and proceed to Entebbe.

Upon arrival, you will be dropped at your preferred location.

Meal Plan: Breakfast and Lunch at Kabalega DinersDrop-off: Preferred location
