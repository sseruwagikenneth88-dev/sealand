Explore Uganda with Sea Land Safaris

Discover the heart of Africa on this 14-day journey through Uganda – the Pearl of Africa. This itinerary is designed for travelers seeking a balance of adventure, comfort, and cultural immersion. You’ll track gorillas and chimpanzees in their natural habitats, enjoy spectacular game drives across savannah plains, witness the mighty Murchison Falls, and relax on boat cruises. Along the way, you’ll experience Uganda’s warm hospitality, unique traditions, and breathtaking landscapes. With expert guides, comfortable hand-picked accommodations, and seamless transfers, every detail is crafted to give you a truly memorable safari.

Day by day

Detailed itinerary

Day 1: Entebbe to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary- Rhino Tracking

Welcome to Uganda, the Pearl of Africa! Upon your arrival, a professional SEA LAND SAFARIS guide will warmly welcome you and pick you up from your preferred location in Kampala. Your adventure begins with a scenic drive through the lush countryside as you head north toward the Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary.

Arrive at the lodge, check in, and enjoy a refreshing lunch. After lunch, embark on an exciting guided rhino tracking experience inside the sanctuary. This rare opportunity allows you to get remarkably close to the endangered southern white rhinos while learning about the conservation efforts that protect them.

As you walk through the vast plains accompanied by trained rangers, expect to encounter other wildlife species and beautiful birdlife. Later return to the lodge and spend the rest of the afternoon relaxing, taking in the tranquility of the sanctuary. Later, enjoy a delicious dinner served in a serene bush environment before retiring for the night.

Activities: Rhino trackingAccommodation: Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 2: Shoebill Trekking

After an early breakfast, set out for an exciting Shoebill Trekking experience—one of the most sought-after birds encounters in Africa. Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary is one of the few places in Uganda where the majestic Shoebill Stork can reliably be seen in its natural wetland habitat.

Your trek begins with a guided walk through the sanctuary’s lush vegetation and into the swampy areas where the Shoebill is commonly found. Depending on water levels, the experience may include a short canoe excursion deeper into the marsh, increasing your chances of spotting this prehistoric-looking bird.

The Shoebill, known for its towering height, powerful bill, and slow, graceful movements, is a rare and unforgettable sight. As you explore the wetlands, keep an eye out for other spectacular water birds, including herons, egrets, African jacanas, and swamp-dwellers that thrive in this ecosystem.

After your Shoebill Trek, return to the lodge for lunch and spend the afternoon relaxing around the swimming pool or enjoying the peaceful sanctuary surroundings.

Enjoy dinner and overnight at the lodge.

Activities: Shoebill Trekking (Wetland walk + canoe exploration depending on the season)Accommodation: Ziwa Rhino & Wildlife RanchMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 3: Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary to Visit Top of the falls

After breakfast, depart from Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary and drive to Murchison Falls National Park (2 hrs). En route, visit the Top of the falls, where the mighty Nile River plunges 43m through a 7m gorge. Feel the ground tremble beneath your feet as you witness the power of the world’s longest river.

After check-in at the lodge and, embark on an evening game drive across the savannah. Expect to see elephants, giraffes, Jackson’s hartebeest, and with luck, lions or leopards or Spend the afternoon relaxing at the lodge, perhaps by the swimming pool.

Activities: Top of the Falls & evening game driveAccommodation: Paraa safari Lodge/ Fort Murchison lodge(Midrange)& Nile safari Lodge/Entikko Safari Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board

Day 4: Game Drive and Boat cruise on Nile River

Start early (6:30 am) with a sunrise game drive on the Buligi Circuit, one of East Africa’s most scenic safari routes. Look for predators like lions returning from a night hunt, herds of buffalo, Rothschild giraffes, Uganda kobs, and warthogs.

After lunch, take a boat cruise on the Nile to the base of Murchison Falls. Along the banks, watch hippos wallowing, crocodiles sunbathing, and elephants drinking. Birdlife is spectacular – keep an eye out for the rare shoebill stork. Later proceed to the Lodge as you do an evening game drive. Enjoy dinner and rest for the night.

Activities: Morning game drive and boat cruiseAccommodation: Paraa safari Lodge/ Fort Murchison lodge(Midrange)& Nile safari Lodge/Entikko Safari Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half BoardMeal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Red chilli Restaurant

Day 5: Scenic Drive to Fort Portal

After breakfast, travel southwest to Fort Portal, a charming town nestled in the foothills of the Rwenzori Mountains (5–6 hrs). Pass through tea plantations, crater lakes, and traditional villages. Stop for lunch at Gardens Restaurant in Fort Portal.

Evening at leisure at your lodge with views of crater lakes or the Rwenzori.

Accommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Turaco tree tops(Budget&Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge/Primate Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board (Lunch at Gardens Restaurant)

Day 6: Chimpanzee Tracking – Kibale Forest

After breakfast, drive to Kibale Forest National Park, the “Primate Capital of the World” (home to 13 primate species).

Join a ranger briefing, then trek into the forest to observe wild chimpanzees. Watch them swinging through trees, grooming, and playing. Other primates here include red colobus monkeys and grey-cheeked mangabeys.

Return to your lodge for Lunch and head back for Bigodi swamp walk and visit the local community too. Later enjoy dinner at the lodge and rest for the night.

Activities: Chimpanzee tracking (2–4 hrs trek), Bigodi swamp walk and Visit the local community.Accommodation: Kibale Forest Camp/Turaco tree tops(Budget&Mid-range),Chimpundu Lodge/Primate Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 7: Lake Nkuruba & Queen Elizabeth NP

After breakfast, proceed to Visit Lake Nkuruba, a Crater Lake known for monkeys (black-and-white colobus, red colobus, vervets) and over 130 bird species. Lunch enroute in Kasese and later, transfer to Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Check in at the Lodge and refresh.

In the evening, enjoy an evening game drive around the park, and thereafter return to the lodge for dinner and rest for the night.

Activities: Bird watching, primates and evening game driveAccommodation: Bush Lodge/Buffalo Safari Lodge(Budget & Midrange)Mweya safari lodge/Elephant plains Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board and Lunch in Kasese

Day 8: Morning game Drive and Boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel

Enjoy breakfast at 6:30am at the lodge and embark on a morning game drive in Queen Elizabeth National Park for around 4-5hours, exploring the park's diverse ecosystems and spotting wildlife such as lions, elephants, waterbucks, warthogs, antelopes and other bird species.

Head back to the lodge for lunch and relaxation.

Later in the afternoon, take a boat cruise along the Kazinga Channel- one of the world’s densest concentrations of hippos, which connect Lake Edward and Lake George. The channel is home to a wide variety of wildlife, including hippos, crocodiles, and birds. Here you will enjoy the stunning scenery and wildlife viewing opportunities along the channel

After head back to the lodge for dinner and overnight. Spend the evening relaxing and reflecting on the day's adventures.

Activities: Game drive and Boat cruise along Kazinga channelAccommodation: Bush Lodge/ Mweya safari lodgeMeal Plan: Full Board

Day 9: Transfer to Ishasha sector for Tree-Climbing Lions

Enjoy an early morning breakfast at the lodge, and then proceed to Ishasha sector. Here, lions have developed the unique behavior of climbing fig trees. Relax at your lodge and enjoy lunch, and later in the evening, embark on an evening game drive.

Return to the lodge and enjoy your dinner, rest for the night.

Activities: Track the tree-climbing lions and evening game driveAccommodation: Enjojo lodge(Budget & Midrange)/Ishasha wilderness camp(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 10: Bush Breakfast & Short Game Drive

Start the day with a bush breakfast in the park – coffee and fresh fruit as the sun rises over the savannah. After, enjoy a short game drive as you transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National park. Drive 3–4 hrs to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site ,Check in at Ride 4 a woman a famous lodge in Buhoma sector, enjoy Lunch and Relax at the lodge. In the evening, have dinner and call it a night.

Activities: Bush breakfast & short game driveAccommodation: Ride 4 a Woman/Bakiga Lodge(Budget& Mid-range)/Mahogany springs, Four Gorillas Lodge (Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 11: Gorilla Tracking

After an early breakfast, attend a ranger briefing before your gorilla trek. Trekking may take 2–6 hrs depending on gorilla location, but the reward is immense: one magical hour with a mountain gorilla family.

Return to the lodge for lunch, relax after your trek and then transfer to L.Bunyonyi. Enjoy and relax at the resort. In the evening, enjoy your dinner and rest for the night.

Activities: Gorilla tracking (2–6 hrs trek)Accommodation: Bird nest resort(Budget& Mid-range) & Arcadia Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Ride 4 a Woman

Day 12: Hike around Lake Bunyonyi, boat cruise and visit the Bunyonyi View

After breakfast at the resort, Embark on a guided hike around Lake Bunyonyi, exploring the scenic trails and taking in the breathtaking views.

Proceed to take a boat cruise on Lake Bunyonyi, visiting some of the surrounding islands and learning about the local culture in that area.

Later visit the Bunyonyi View, a scenic viewpoint offering stunning views of the lake and surrounding hills.

Enjoy lunch at the resort and spend the afternoon relaxing and unwinding at the resort, have dinner at the resort and call it a night and get a good rest.

Activities: Hike around Lake Bunyonyi, boat cruise and visit the Bunyonyi ViewAccommodation: Bird nest resort(Budget& Mid-range) & Arcadia Lodge(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

Day 13: Transfer to Lake Mburo National Park

Enjoy breakfast at Bird Nest Resort and after depart for Lake Mburo National Park (approximately 4-5 hour drive).

Have a stop for lunch at Igongo hotel, a cultural center and hotel that showcases Ugandan culture and tradition.

Then proceed to Lake Mburo National Park and check-in at a lodge, spend the afternoon relaxing and unwinding at the lodge, taking in the stunning views of the surrounding landscape. Enjoy dinner at the lodge and rest for the night.

Accommodation: Eagles nest Lodge/Rwakobo Rock(Budget & Mid-range)/Mihingo lodge/Kigambira Safari LodgeMeal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Igongo hotel

Day 14: Return to Entebbe

Enjoy breakfast at the Eagles’ nest camp in Lake Mburo National Park. Embark on a morning game drive in the park, exploring the scenic trails and spotting wildlife such as zebras, impalas, and birds. After, depart for Kampala (approximately 4-5 hour drive)

Stop for lunch at the Equator Restaurant, located at the Uganda Equator, a famous landmark marking the spot where the equator passes through Uganda, take photos and keep those memories

Then proceed driving to Kampala, passing through scenic landscapes and rural villages. Arrive in Entebbe in the late afternoon and the guide will drop you off at your preferred location.

Activities: Morning game driveMeal plan: Lunch at the Equator
