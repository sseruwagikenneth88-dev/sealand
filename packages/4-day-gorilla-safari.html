Day by day

Detailed itinerary

DAY 1: ENTEBBE TO BWINDI (BUHOMA SECTOR)

Early Morning, your professional driver-guide will pick you up from your hotel or residence in Entebbe. After a short briefing about the safari, you will begin your journey to southwestern Uganda. You will travel through central Uganda, passing local towns, markets, and countryside landscapes.

Stop over at Equator Crossing in Kayabwe for a short break, take memorable photos standing in both hemispheres or optional coffee/tea break and proceed.

Enjoy a hot lunch at Igongo cultural center. After continue your journey through the beautiful Kigezi Highlands often referred to as the “Switzerland of Africa” with rolling hills, terraced farms, and scenic valleys.

Arrive at Bwindi Impenetrable National Park (Buhoma sector) and Check-in at your booked accommodation preferably Ride 4 A Woman and freshen up.

Enjoy dinner at the lodge and get early rest in preparation for trekking.

Accommodation: Ride 4 A Woman/Buhoma Lodge (Budget&Mid-range) Gorilla Forest Lodge/Mahogany springs (Luxury)Meal Plan: Half Board and Lunch at Igongo Cultural center

DAY 2: GORILLA TREKKING + BATWA CULTURAL EXPERIENCE

Wake up and enjoy an early breakfast, then Carry packed lunch, drinking water, and trekking gear and transfer to Park Headquarters (7:30 – 8:00 AM)at the briefing point.

Meet Uganda Wildlife Authority rangers and receive guidelines on trekking and interacting with gorillas in group allocation (maximum 8 people per gorilla family)

Enter the dense rainforest with experienced guides and trek duration varies from 2 to 6 hours depending on the gorilla family location

Along the way you will learn about forest plants, birds, and wildlife as you enjoy the adventure of navigating the jungle

Highlight Moment

Once the gorillas are found:

Spend 1 full hour observing them

Watch feeding, grooming, and social interactions

Take photos (no flash allowed)

After trekking, return to the lodge for refreshment, relaxation.

Later visit the indigenous Batwa people (original forest dwellers) where you will Experience traditional dances and songs, Storytelling and demonstration of forest survival skills. This is a meaningful cultural experience supporting local communities

Thereafter, return to lodge, enjoy dinner and overnight

Activities: Gorilla trekking and Visit the indigenous Batwa peopleAccommodation: Ride 4 A Woman/Buhoma community Haven Lodge (Budget&Mid-range), Buhoma Lodge/Mahogany springs (Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

DAY 3: TRANSFER TO LAKE BUNYONYI

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the lodge and drive to Lake Bunyonyi. The journey offers scenic views of hills and terraced landscape.

Arrive and check into your lakeside lodge, enjoy lunch with views of the lake at bird nest Resort.

In the afternoon, embark on canoe experience. Paddle across calm waters with a guide and explore nearby islands.

Head for Island Hiking to visit one of the islands on a Short guided walk with panoramic views then later Sunset Boat Ride perfect for photography and unwinding.

In the evening have dinner at the lodge as you enjoy peaceful views of Lake Bunyonyi.

Activities: Hiking, Visit the L.Bunyonyi Island and Sunset boat rideAccommodation: Bird Nest Resort (Budget& Midrange)/Arcadia Lodges /Bunyonyi Luxury Resort(Luxury)Meal Plan: Full Board

DAY 4: LAKE BUNYONYI TO ENTEBBE

After morning breakfast at the lodge, check-out of the Resort and drive back towards Entebbe as you enjoy scenic countryside views.

Stop for lunch at the Equator and get another photo moments and souvenir shopping and then continue to Entebbe.

On arrival, drop-off at hotel or at Entebbe international airport.

Meal Plan: Breakfast and Lunch at Equator
